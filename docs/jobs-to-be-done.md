# Jobs To Be Done

Both jobs belong to the **Group Diner** role defined in [app-roles.md](app-roles.md).

## J-1: Collect what I am owed after paying for the group

**Role:** Group Diner

When I have paid the whole restaurant bill for a group that agreed to split it equally, I want to work out exactly how much each of the others owes me, including their part of the tax and tip, so I can ask everyone for the right amount once and get back everything I paid beyond my own part, no more and no less.

## J-2: Pay back my exact part when someone else paid

**Role:** Group Diner

When someone else in my group has paid a restaurant bill we agreed to split equally, I want to know my exact part of it, including tax and tip, so I can pay them back the right amount on the spot without overpaying, underpaying or arguing over a few cents.
